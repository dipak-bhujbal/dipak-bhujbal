### Llama Tools · Model training and evaluation

An independent Llama 3.1 8B study using LoRA supervised fine-tuning and DPO for structured tool calling. I investigate tool selection, argument correctness, evaluation integrity, and whether a further training recipe earns an upgrade.

The reconciled study reports **81.75% to 91.50% on 400 `simple_python` cases**, using a custom scorer over BFCL examples. Argument correctness drove the gain; two DPO follow-ups did not improve on SFT. This is an endpoint-level result, with the measurement and capability limits documented alongside the experiment.

[Versioned research evidence](https://huggingface.co/datasets/centuriandip/llama-3.1-8b-tools-dpo-v2-evidence)

### Agent-safety research · Ask what the test can establish

**Boundary-Validity** is a separate research project examining the validity of agentic boundary-crossing evaluations, with a protocol, evaluation framework, and design documentation.

My newer **Boundary-Crossing** study asks whether an agent chose not to cross a boundary or never discovered the route. It separates discovery from crossing, examines selection effects, and proposes experiments with explicit identification limits. Research design and instrument diagnostics remain distinct from completed treatment findings.

[Earlier Boundary-Validity preregistration](https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/2KH7B)
